Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your The Match account from within the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to The Match.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose “Delete my account permanently”.
  4. Confirm your choice and type DELETE when asked.

Account deletion is permanent. Your account and associated database records will be deleted.

Can't sign in?

If you cannot access your account, contact support to request deletion. Provide the email address linked to your account so ownership can be verified.

Never send your password, bank PIN, or verification code.
