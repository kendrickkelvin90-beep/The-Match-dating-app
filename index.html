ℹ️ About The Match

The Match is a dating and connection app for adults aged 18 and over. It helps members discover people, express interest, find mutual likes, and start conversations when both people are interested.

Discover: Explore member profiles and choose whether you are interested in someone.

Likes and matches: Send likes and connect with people who like you back. Seeing who likes you may require Premium.

Messages: Chat with your connections when messaging is available for your match.

Your profile: Keep your profile details up to date so other members can learn about you. Location sharing is optional and can be managed in your profile settings.

Premium: Access additional features offered in the app. Visit the Premium section to review available plans and payment status.

Privacy and respect: Respect other members, communicate honestly, and respect consent and personal boundaries. Never share another person's private information without permission.

Safety: The Match is for adults 18+ only. Be careful when sharing personal details. If you arrange to meet someone, consider a public place and tell someone you trust about your plans.

The Match helps people connect, but members are responsible for their own interactions and decisions. Features may change as the app improves.
