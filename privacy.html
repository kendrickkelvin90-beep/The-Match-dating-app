Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

1. Information we collect

The Match may process account email addresses, profile details, photos, dating preferences, likes, matches, messages, location information when permitted, safety reports, and information submitted for Premium payment review.

2. How information is used

We use information to operate accounts, display profiles, provide matching and messaging, review payment requests, respond to safety reports, and protect the service.

3. Service providers

The Match uses Supabase for backend services and Render for website hosting. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

4. Profile visibility

Information you add to your dating profile may be visible to other members. Avoid publishing private contact details or information you do not want other members to see.

5. Location

Location access is optional and depends on your device and browser permissions. Some location-based features may not work if you deny permission.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained when needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, address safety issues, or resolve payment disputes.

7. Account deletion

You can request account deletion using our Delete Account page. Some records may need to be retained for legal or legitimate security purposes.

8. Adults only

The Match is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when our service or data practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact The Match support team on WhatsApp.
